# Tripadvisor award artwork

Published copies of the original year-specific Tripadvisor Travelers' Choice artwork replace the previously redrawn presentation badges. No year labels, logos, colors or layouts have been redrawn. The 2026 asset uses the centered green arch lockup. Original aspect ratios and transparency are preserved.

Source URLs, conversion notes, pixel dimensions and checksums are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json). These copies were obtained from award-recipient publishers, not from a signed-in Tripadvisor Management Center. The artwork is generic; the owner's confirmation of this business's award years is separately recorded in the backend content manifest.
