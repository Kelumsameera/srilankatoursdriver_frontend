# Page banner photo credits

Fallback banners for inner pages (used only until a hero image is uploaded in the admin).
The credited source photos below come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons Attribution licences,
which allow commercial use with credit. The vehicles page now uses the visibly labeled generated illustrative
image in `../editorial/vehicle-private-transfer-illustrative.webp`; the older licensed vehicles banner remains
in this folder. Each source photo was resized and converted to WebP (`name.webp` full width,
`name-1080.webp`, `name-640.webp`); the tours banner was also lightly denoised. No other edits.
The credit is shown on the banner itself (see `src/lib/page-banners.ts`); keep both in sync.
